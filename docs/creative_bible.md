# Channel Creative Bible — DRAFT v0.1

> Working title: **रहस्य की बातें — रामायण** (final channel name still to be decided)
> Status: draft for Gate 1. The visual section will be revised once the style reference images arrive.

## 1. Positioning
A **mystery-driven, research-backed** Hindu mythology storytelling channel. Every episode opens one lesser-known chapter, tells it as a story, and is honest about its source.

**Promise to the viewer:** *"रामायण की इस कहानी के बारे में मुझे पहले पता ही नहीं था — और ये सच में ग्रंथ में है."*

**Guiding principle:** Mystery → curiosity. Research → credibility. Storytelling → emotional connection.

## 2. Audience
Hindi-speaking viewers aged 15–40: students, young professionals, mythology fans and families. The art should be gentle enough for children, and the storytelling serious enough for adults.

## 3. Voice and tone of writing
- Natural spoken Hindi with light Hinglish, written in Devanagari.
- Reverent, never preachy. The tone is curious, never sensational.
- The first line is always a specific question or image. Never use "नमस्कार दोस्तों".
- Short sentences and room to breathe. One idea per line of narration.
- Approved phrasings: "एक कम सुनी जाने वाली कथा", "वाल्मीकि रामायण के अनुसार…", "कुछ परम्पराओं में…", "लोककथा कहती है…"
- Banned phrasings: "दुनिया से छिपाया गया", "वैज्ञानिकों ने साबित किया", "100% सच", "किसी को नहीं पता". The last is allowed only if it is literally true.

## 4. Story structure (target duration 60 s, range 45–75 s)
| Stage | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Question | 0–5 s | A specific, visual hook question |
| Mystery | 5–15 s | Who or what, and why it matters |
| Reveal | 15–40 s | Cause, then effect, then motive |
| Turning point | 40–55 s | The strongest revelation, which answers the hook |
| Ending | 55–70 s | An insight or a reflective question, plus an on-screen source card |

**Source card:** Every video ends with an on-screen line such as *"स्रोत: वाल्मीकि रामायण, सुन्दरकाण्ड, सर्ग 27"*. This is our credibility signature.

## 5. Visual identity (provisional — your references will override this)
- **Look:** An illustrated Indian storybook brought to life. Graphite or pencil outlines, organic imperfect line, soft watercolour washes, visible paper grain.
- **Palette (provisional):**
  - Base: warm paper ivory `#F3E9D2`, indigo-night `#1E2A4A`, forest green `#2F4A3A`.
  - Accents: saffron `#E39B2D` and vermilion `#B5382A`, both used sparingly.
  - Divine light: soft gold `#F2D27A`.
- **Lighting:** Painterly, motivated by a source (diya, moon, dawn), with atmospheric depth.
- **Characters:** Natural proportions with gentle stylization. Expressive eyes. Consistent jewelry and costume taken from locked character sheets.
- **Avoid:** A 3D or plastic look, glossy skin, chibi proportions, invented religious symbols, fake Sanskrit glyphs, flashing effects, and a constant zoom on still images.
- **Motion vocabulary:** Blinks, breathing, cloth and hair moving in wind, firelight flicker, falling leaves, layered parallax, slow motivated camera pushes. Faster motion is reserved for action beats.

## 6. Sound identity
- A **single consistent narrator voice**: warm, deep, a little mysterious, natural Hindi.
- Music: tanpura or drone beds, bansuri, soft strings, light percussion for tension. The music is ducked under the voice (target about −20 to −24 dB under the narration).
- **Silence before the reveal:** a deliberate 0.5–1.0 s gap.
- Licensing: all music and SFX must be original, royalty-free or properly licensed for commercial use. No film scores.

## 7. Subtitles
Devanagari, a bold readable sans (Noto Sans Devanagari or a similar typeface), placed inside the 9:16 safe area (above the bottom ~20%, clear of the right-side UI). Two lines at most, with keywords highlighted in saffron.

## 8. Accuracy charter
1. Every claim is traced to a text and a location (kanda and sarga), or it is labelled as a tradition or folklore.
2. No fabricated verses or quotes.
3. Differences between versions are presented with respect. We don't "debunk" devotion.
4. A fact-check file is kept for every video in `research/`.

## 9. Brand don'ts
No imitation of another channel's scripts, art, voice or scenes. No cloned celebrity voices. No automatic publishing.
