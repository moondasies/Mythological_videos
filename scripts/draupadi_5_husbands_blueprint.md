## 1. Topic research and why the hook works

### What the Mahabharata says
| Claim used in the video | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| In a previous birth Draupadi was a **sage's daughter**. She did tapas, and Shiva (Shankara) offered a boon. She asked for "a husband with all virtues" **five times**, so Shiva said she would get **five husbands in her next birth**. | Mahabharata, Adi Parva. Vyasa tells this to the Pandavas before the swayamvara (Chaitraratha sub-parva, Critical Ed. ~1.157) and again to Drupada (Vaivahika sub-parva, Critical Ed. ~1.189). | Core story is textual ✅. Exact chapter/verse numbers still to verify 🔎 |
| Arjuna, disguised as a brahmin, wins Draupadi at the swayamvara by hitting the target. | Adi Parva, Swayamvara sub-parva | Textual ✅ |
| The Pandavas lived in a **potter's house**. They announced "we have brought alms (bhiksha)", and Kunti, **without looking**, said to share it all together. | Adi Parva (Critical Ed. ~1.182) 🔎 | Textual ✅ |
| Drupada objected that it was against dharma. Vyasa came and explained it was ordained. | Adi Parva, Vaivahika sub-parva (Critical Ed. ~1.187–189) 🔎 | Textual ✅ |
| Draupadi is dark-complexioned ("Krishnaa"). | Adi Parva, her birth from the fire altar | Textual ✅. Her character design must reflect this |
| **"Fish eye" target** | Popular depiction; the text describes a target hit through a revolving device | ⚠️ Popular. Visual is fine, but the VO says only "लक्ष्य" (target) |
| **Each Pandava holds one of the virtues she asked for** (dharma, strength, archery, beauty, wisdom) | Popular interpretation | ⚠️ Not in the text, so the VO says "कहते हैं" (it is said) |
| Her past-life name **Nalayani / Indrasena** (wife of Maudgalya) | Linked in some versions and retellings; disputed | ❌ Not used in the VO |
| The **Five Indras** secret (Indra imprisoned by Shiva; the Pandavas are five Indras and Draupadi is Shri) | Adi Parva, Panchendra-upakhyana (Critical Ed. ~1.189) 🔎 | Textual. Saved for **Episode 2** |

### Why it triggers curiosity
1. **Paradox in one line:** "She asked for **one** husband and got **five**?" The viewer has to resolve the contradiction.
2. **Familiar and unknown at once:** Almost everyone knows Draupadi had five husbands. Very few know the past-life boon, which delivers the "मुझे पता ही नहीं था" (I never knew this) effect.
3. **Double twist:** Most people think Kunti's slip was the cause. The video shows Kunti's words were only the trigger, and the real cause lies in the previous birth.
4. **A countable payoff:** "एक बार… दो… तीन… चार… पाँच" (once, two, three, four, five) is rhythmic, visual and easy to remember.
5. **Debate-ready ending:** "वरदान या श्राप?" (boon or curse?) gets one-word comments, which are easy to write and help the algorithm.
6. **Sequel hook:** The Five Indras secret sets up Part 2.

### Reference analysis (your uploaded reel, viewed frame by frame; audio not heard)
- **Style:** Flat vector characters with bold dark outlines, 1–2 tone cel shading, large eyes, gold ornaments and full-body poses.
- **Backgrounds:** Gradient night or dusk skies (purple, gold, maroon), light rays, particles, and orbit rings around deities.
- **Labels:** Thin white or gold Hindi labels in the top third ("शनिदेव", "छाया", "विनाशकारी दृष्टि", "कमेंट करें").
- **Pacing:** A new composition roughly every 2–4 s, mostly reframing the same character. Eyes glow at power moments.
- **What we take:** The pacing, the label system and the glow FX.
- **What we change:** Our own palette, our own character designs, heavier labels with a saffron outline, and real limb animation rather than mostly static poses. We do not copy their characters or scenes.


### 3e. Rigging and animation workflow
1. Approve each character design. Then generate or trace a **parts sheet**: head, eyes (open, half, closed), 3–4 mouth shapes, torso, upper and lower arms, hands (open, fist, namaste), legs and clothing layers.
   - Image models do not reliably produce clean, separable parts.
   - Plan on a **vector trace and clean-up** pass in Illustrator or Inkscape.
2. Rig the parts as a 2D puppet in **Adobe Character Animator** (auto lip-sync from the VO), **After Effects with Duik**, **Moho** or **Spine**.
3. Use lip-sync from the final VO. Add blinks every 3–5 s, breathing idle and cloth secondary motion.
4. Camera: change the framing every 2.5–3.5 s, following the shot list.
5. Export 1080×1920, 30 fps. Keep labels inside the top 15–30% safe band and clear of the right-side UI.
